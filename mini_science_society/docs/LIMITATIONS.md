# Limitations

- The four worlds are two law families with two parameter cases each, not four
  independent scientific theories.
- IG measures discrimination among a frozen candidate set; it is not a complete
  measure of scientific value.
- The source condition is represented by synthetic observations and does not
  establish cross-disciplinary transfer in human science.
- Mock outputs validate routing and logging only. They are `NON_EVIDENCE`.
- A four-world, one-replicate pilot cannot support statistical significance or a
  general claim that a Society is superior to an equal-budget single-agent
  baseline.
- If the legacy provider does not return a cost receipt, cost is an estimate or
  `null`, and budget equality is marked `BUDGET_NOT_VERIFIED`.
